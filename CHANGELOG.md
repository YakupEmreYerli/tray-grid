# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-19

- First version: a StatusNotifierHost of its own, a from-scratch dbusmenu client, and an icon-grid popup for the Plasma 6 panel.
- Left, middle, wheel and right click behave like Plasma's tray; icons and items update live.
- Settings: columns, icon size, passive items, close on activate, per-app visibility.
- End-to-end test on a private D-Bus session.
